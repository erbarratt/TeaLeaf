# AI systems (`Assets/Scripts/AI/`, namespace `AI`)

Guards; roadmap Phase 6. **Built, working in headset** (test guard from the menu), model animation and the perception numbers (suspicion, search, hearing, vision zones, chase and catch range) included.

## The guard

- **`GuardState`**: `Patrol`, `Suspicious`, `Search`, `Chase`, `Stunned`, `KnockedOut`. **Nothing kills a guard.**
- **`Guard`** (root, `[RequireComponent(NavMeshAgent)]`, `INoiseListener`, `IDebugDrawable`): switch-on-`State` machine, senses, sounds. **Own `Update()`**, outside player tick order. Moves via agent (`GoTo()`, `Stop()`, `HasArrived()`); turns itself only standing (`FaceTowards()`).
  - **Turn before walk:** `GoTo()` from standing (agent < 0.1 m/s) sets `isStopped`; `TickTurningToWalk()` (each frame, before state tick) turns on the spot to `steeringTarget`, releases within 15°; feet stepped by `GuardFootLock` (no turn clip). All set-offs use it; `GoTo()` while walking (chase renewal) carries on; `Stop()` cancels a turn.
  - **Turns on the spot ease in and out** (`TurnTowards()`, every standing turn: set-off, facing a stimulus or body, back to post, search look-about): speed rises at `turnAcceleration` 2880°/s² to at most `turnSpeed` (720 on the prefab) and is capped at sqrt(2 x accel x angle left) so it stops on the target (trapezoid speed profile; half turn ~0.5s, peaks at `turnSpeed`). Speed-based, so a moving target needs no restart; a turn not continued from the previous frame starts from still.
  - **Eases to a stop on arrival** (`TickArrivalBraking()`, every frame after the state tick): agent speed = state's cruise speed (`patrolSpeed`/`chaseSpeed`, set on state entry into `_cruiseSpeed`, never `_agent.speed` directly) capped at sqrt(2 x `brakeDeceleration` 3 x (remainingDistance - stopping)); run stops over ~2.2m/1.2s, walk ~0.2m. Not in chase (runs up to the player).
  - **Two meters, 0-1, as Thief VR's**: `Suspicion` (full -> patrol becomes Suspicious) and `Detection` (full -> Chase from any state). Each holds after it was last raised (`RaiseSuspicion()`/`RaiseDetection()` stamp the time), then drains: suspicion `suspicionHoldTime` 1s then `suspicionDecayRate` 1/s; detection `detectionHoldTime` 4s then `detectionDecayRate` 0.25/s, not in chase. `RaiseToInvestigate()` (bump, bolt, body found, coming round from a stun/KO, chase lost): suspicion full, detection at least `investigateThreshold` 0.5 (Thief's). `ClearMeters()` on giving up, a shrugged-off suspicion, KO.
  - **Sight** `Look()`, not per frame: `lookInterval` 0.15s (random per-guard offset); `farLookInterval` 0.5s, no rays, when the player's head is > `GuardSight.Reach` (33m) + 2m from the eye. **`GuardSight`** (ScriptableObject, `Assets/Data/AI/GuardSight.asset`, on the prefab's `sight`; shared, Play Mode edits kept) holds **Thief VR's four vision zones** (from its `Guard_Data_Default` and `VT_*` meshes; theirs sit at 1.8m on the body, ours on the eye):
    - **One view frustum split by rules in code**: Peripheral is the frustum, the other zones sit inside it. Each `Zone` = `range` ahead; `halfAngle` from the eye capped at `maxHalfWidth`; `upAngle`/`maxAbove`, `downAngle`/`maxBelow` likewise; past `narrowFrom` each limit eases to `endHalfWidth`/`endAbove`/`endBelow` at `range` (`narrowFrom` >= `range` = no narrowing). Inside = within the limits at the point's depth (`IsInside()`, `GetLimits()`), no physics. **Very Close** 3m, 68° to ±1.5m, 2.1m below to 0.95m above, narrowing from 1.8m to ±0.5m; **Close** 6m, 71.5° (~144° across) to ±4.75m, 3.8m below to 1.5m above, narrowing from 3.7m to ±2.1m, ±1.25m; **Direct Line Of Sight** 30m, 29° (~58°) to ±5m (then a 10m corridor), 4m below to 2m above; **Peripheral** 30m, 77° (~154°) to ±12.5m, 6.8m below to 2m above. Fitted to Thief's meshes: each zone keeps 97-100% of its mesh's volume; Very Close is ~11% bigger, Peripheral ~15% (wider at 5-20m: an angle plus cap can't follow Thief's slow widening to 20m).
    - Each zone's `Band`s follow on out from the eye (`length`; distance = straight from eye). Per band a `lit` and `dark` `Fill`: `suspicionTime`/`detectionTime` (seconds to fill from empty; < 0 = never), still/crouch multipliers per meter, `movingMultiplier`, `fastMultiplier` above `fastSpeed`. Times blend across a band to the next band's (not with a never). Multipliers scale the time (2.5 = slower, 0.02 = 50x faster); **a multiplier of 0 = never** (inferred: Thief's dark 3.75-6m still/crouched suspicion). Dark is never past 6m and never in Peripheral; dark close up, moving fast (> 1.5-3 m/s) is nearly instant (x0.02-0.05).
    - Lit = `PlayerVisibility.Visibility` >= `litThreshold` 0.3 (Visibility includes crouch: crouched in moonlight 0.24 = dark, so only seen within 6m). Still = `PlayerVisibility.IsStill` (`MovementState` `Still`/`CrouchStill`: stick not moving the body; room-scale steps and sway don't count, sliding/falling (`Airborne`), climbing and mantling are moving); fast = `PlayerVisibility.Speed` > `fastSpeed`. Crouched = `PlayerVisibility.IsCrouched`. Where zones overlap the fastest wins, per meter (`TryGetFillTimes()`, false = no zone can see it, so no ray). How Thief combines zones isn't in its export: inferred.
    - **Five weighted points** (`PlayerVisibility.GetSightPoint()`/`GetSightWeight()`): head sides and top 0.17, under the head 0.5, waist 0.8 (raised 0.15m crouched). A point counts if a zone can fill on it and `Physics.Linecast` on `sightLayers` (Environment+Interactable, triggers ignored) is clear; showing weight >= `seenWeight` 0.5 = seen; rate = fastest of the showing points. Per meter: fill `elapsed / time`; a meter this view can't fill drains as unseen. **Detection fills only while suspicion is full** (Thief VR's order, inferred from play: crouched in the dark 3.75-6m, where suspicion never fills, Thief's guards never detect though its detection times alone would in 5s); otherwise it holds and drains as unseen. Hearing and `RaiseToInvestigate()` raise detection directly. Up to five rays per look, only for points a zone can see.
  - **Never senses behind it** except noise or **bump** (`HasBumped()`, per look: feet within `bumpRange` 0.5m, level, same floor - over both radii, so arm's-length blackjack isn't a bump): `RaiseToInvestigate()`, stimulus = player, Suspicious. Not in chase.
  - **Hearing** `OnNoiseHeard()` (from `Core.NoiseSystem`, already room/portal-routed): detection + loudness x `hearingGain` 0.7 (`distractionGain` 1.5 noisemaker), suspicion + that x `hearingSuspicionScale` 3.33 (one ordinary 0.45 footstep fills it); heard-from = stimulus. **Ignores guards' noises** (source a guard root, static dictionary) and all noise in chase. In Search, a noise taking detection to `investigateThreshold` moves the search only if > 1.5m away (else a steady noise roots it).
  - **Patrol**: `PatrolRoute` at `patrolSpeed` 1.07; no route = holds placed pose; resumes at nearest point.
  - **Suspicious**: stops, faces stimulus, barks; after `suspiciousTime` 3s -> Search if detection peak >= `investigateThreshold` 0.5, else Patrol **with both meters emptied** (else instant re-trigger).
  - **Search**: walk to place, look about `searchLookTime` 2.5s (`TickLookAbout()`: holds a heading `LookAboutHold` 0.8s, then a `TurnTowards()` turn 60-120° the other way from the last; never leaves mid-turn), then `searchPointCount` 3 random spots within `searchRadius` 4m (`NavMesh.SamplePosition`), then give up (bark) -> Patrol; or `searchTimeout` 30s, x`alertedSearchScale` (2) once any body found.
  - **Chase**: `chaseSpeed` 3.6; destination renewed per look while seen. **Caught** = seen, within `catchRange` 1.1m level, same floor -> `LevelManager.Caught()`. Unseen `chaseLostTime` 4s -> Search from last seen.
  - **Bolts** (the bow's arrows, from `Player.PlayerArrows`, which holds the durations). `Guard.FindByBody(collider)` static dictionary of the capsule and every `GuardRagdoll` hit box (bolts hit the hit boxes; their mask leaves out the `Guard` layer). `IsHead(collider)`: the hit box is the ragdoll's head. `Stun(seconds)`: any state but KO, chase included -> `Stunned` (agent stopped, no looking/bump/body finding, noises ignored, no footsteps; re-stun restarts); expiry (`TickStunned()`): `RaiseToInvestigate()`, searches where it stands, look timer reset. `Sedate(seconds)`: not a state, timer at top of `Update()` in any state, then `KnockOut()`; unnoticed; 2nd dart doesn't restart. `HitByBolt(shotFrom)` (water, noisemaker, rope, stun to body): `RaiseToInvestigate()`, stimulus = shot-from; patrol -> Suspicious (-> search there), Search moves there; nothing if chasing/stunned/down.
  - **Knock-out** `TryKnockOut(attackerPosition)` (from `Player.PlayerBlackjack`): down if stunned (any side) or not chasing and attacker behind (facing · level attacker->guard dir >= `behindDot` 0.2); else Chase. `KnockOut()`: `GuardRagdoll.GoLimp(agent velocity)`, agent and both colliders off (or `visual` laid flat without a ragdoll), component disabled. Down until woken. `Guard.FindByHead(collider)` static dictionary.
  - **Testing toggle** `knockedOut` (Inspector, Play Mode): tick knocks out, clear wakes (`WakeUp()`); acted on via `EditorApplication.delayCall` from `OnValidate()`; follows the state (set in `KnockOut()`/`GetUp()`).
  - **Testing toggle** `detectsPlayer` (Inspector, default on, any time): off = no sight of the player or bump (`Look()` only drains and finds bodies), noises ignored, `HitByBolt()` ignored, a failed `TryKnockOut()` doesn't start a chase. A chase under way runs out by `chaseLostTime` (no catch: needs the player seen). Bodies still found and woken.
  - **Bodies**: per look, a KO'd guard whose `BodyPosition` (ragdoll hips while limp, else root + 0.3m) is in view in light >= `bodyLightThreshold` 0.15 (`Core.SceneLight.LevelAt()`) is found: bark, Search with the body first; all searches longer (static flag, cleared when last guard destroyed). **Always wakeable** by an awake guard: within `WakeReach` 1.5m of `BodyPosition`, faces it `wakeTime` 3s (`TickWaking()`), `WakeUp()` (nothing unless down and not already rising): with a ragdoll `GuardRagdoll.StandUp()` and `_isGettingUp` (state stays `KnockedOut`, `Update()` does nothing else until `IsStandingUp` ends), then `GetUp()` undoes `KnockOut()` (`visual` back to pose saved in `Awake()`, senses fresh); woken guard searches where it lay, finder resumes. Claim = body's `_isBodyFound`, released by `DropWakeTarget()` (in `SetState()`, `BeginSearchAt()`) when finder changes state or search moves. Unreachable body stays claimed, never woken.
  - **Sounds**: footsteps `PlayFootstep(isStandingStep)` -> `Core.SurfaceSounds.PlayStep()`, none stunned/KO, noise `footstepNoiseScale` 1 x volume (guards ignore guards' noises: only for noise circles); **timing is the animation's** (`GuardAnimation` walking/running, `GuardFootLock` standing), standing steps at `standingStepVolume` 0.35. Barks `suspiciousBark`, `searchBark`, `gaveUpBark`, `spottedBark`, `foundBodyBark` from head, sound only. Both show on the compass.
  - **Optional `stateRenderer`** (greybox): property-block tint on change - grey/yellow/orange/red/violet/dark for patrol/suspicious/search/chase/stunned/KO. **Empty on the model guard** (darkens texture); state shows in gizmo colour and `GuardDebug`. Optional `visual` (greybox, laid flat on KO) likewise empty (ragdoll falls).
  - Level ended (`LevelManager.State` != `Playing`): stops in place.
  - Gizmo: the four zones' wireframes (a rectangle wherever an outline bends) in state colour (`GuardSight.DrawZones()`), **the zone filling a meter fastest in orange** while the player is seen (from the last look: `TryGetFillTimes()` also gives the zone with the fastest time of either meter; over the showing points the fastest wins; none while stunned/KO; same orange as Search, so not visible in Search), two lines above head scaled by suspicion (yellow) and detection (red, above); selected: line to stimulus.

No rays (no slope/step fit). **Standing steps by code** (agent < `Guard.StandingSpeed` 0.1: facing a stimulus, looking about, turning to set off): each foot held with position and rotation; home = where the animation draws it (turns with the body); held foot > `stepDistance` 0.12m or `stepAngle` 25° from home steps there over `stepTime` 0.25s, eased, arc `stepHeight` 0.08m; one foot at a time, the further out first; each landing -> `Guard.PlayFootstep(true)`. Inactive (unseen/far) but standing, not stunned/KO: a quiet step each `stepAngle`/2 turned, **never two standing steps (seen or unseen) closer than `stepTime`** (a 720°/s turn otherwise sounds one nearly every frame). Coming to a stand, held feet stay, others held where drawn; setting off, a mid-step foot fades into the walk. `IDebugDrawable` (Play Mode): ball per locked foot (green planted, yellow fading, orange stepping), line to animated pos; standing, small cyan ball at each home. Measured slide: 0.14 -> 0.004 m/s walk, 0.69 -> 0.04 run.
