TeaLeaf clean-up review - unused packages, scripts and assets
=============================================================

Written 2026-10-26, to go through with the maintainer before deleting anything.
Nothing has been deleted. Full file-by-file list: CleanupReview_FullList.txt (same folder).

How "used" was decided
----------------------
- Root: the Intro scene only (Assets/Scenes/Intro.unity), plus everything in a Resources
  folder (loaded by name in builds), Assets/Settings, Assets/XR, the input actions and
  TextMesh Pro. Everything those reference, followed all the way down
  (AssetDatabase.GetDependencies, recursive), counts as used.
- Assets a script or the level generator's JSON names by path or GUID are NOT counted as
  used, but are tagged in the full list ([path in code], [folder in code], [in level-gen
  data]): they are what the Dev Room builders, Pose with Hand, the generator and the
  variants tool reach for. Deleting one of those breaks that tool, not the game.
- A Unity build only ever contains what its scenes and Resources reference, so nothing
  below makes the game bigger or slower. The gain is repo size, Dropbox sync, import and
  domain-reload time, and less to read.

4. LEVEL GENERATOR DATA (Assets/Data/LevelGen)
==============================================
- Proxies/ (69 files, 141 MB, git-ignored): "Three Streets" building proxy meshes and
  atlas. Intro uses the three-streets layout, but **no proxy renderer is in the scene
  now** (0 found), so these are left over from an earlier generation. Either rebuild
  proxies for Intro (the generator's building proxies step) or delete the folder. The generator
  should arguably delete its own stale proxies when it rebuilds (a generator change, ask).
- Sheared/ (99 of 134 sheared mesh assets unused, ~4 MB): sheared wall meshes from earlier
  generations, no longer referenced by Intro. The generator makes them on demand; delete
  the unused ones (or the whole folder and regenerate Intro). Same "clean up its own old
  output" question as Proxies.
- Layouts/: example-block, example-quarter, example-slope, example-street,
  example-three-levels, example-two-lots (6 specs, 115 KB). Named in LevelGen/CLAUDE.md
  and GeneratedRoot.cs as examples. Tiny; keep unless the examples are retired.
- catalogue.json, annotations.json, GeneratorSettings.asset, ProxyLightmapParameters.asset:
  used by the generator. Keep.

7. TRIPO MODELS (Assets/TripoModels)
====================================
Every Tripo import keeps its source textures in a .fbm folder. The ones a material
actually uses count as used; the rest are Tripo's extra maps (separate metallic,
roughness, "_rm" packed and "metallic_Smoothness" maps the URP material doesn't read).
- Unused extra maps in used models (about 70 MB): guard_1 (6 files, 16 MB, incl. an
  older rgb/normal pair), wooden_door (rm 6 MB, roughness, metallic), rusted_kettle
  (rm 11.9 MB, roughness, metallic, and the unused rusted_kettle_3d_model.fbx - the scene
  uses kettle.fbx), wooden_barrel (rm 7 MB, roughness, metallic_Smoothness, the unused
  wooden_barrel_3d_model.fbx, and ladder.fbx + ladder_LOD1/2.asset filed in the barrel's
  folder), lock, lockpicks, cudgel, armored_gauntlet (its .fbx too: the hand uses
  Art/Models/hand.fbx), arrows (the combined arrows_3d_model.fbx: the game uses the six
  split ones), treasure_chest (metallic, roughness).
  Deleting an .fbm texture Tripo Bridge would re-download on a re-import is harmless.
- bow_3d_model: bow_3d_model.fbx (Tripo source), bow_rigged.fbx (the script's output,
  the handed copies are made from it), bow_rigged_hole.fbx (an older try). Delete
  bow_rigged_hole; keep the other two as the pipeline's sources (Player/CLAUDE.md
  describes both).
- Whole models in no scene and no prefab: blue_floral_porcelain (12 MB),
  coin_with_raven (23 MB), green_glass_bottle (24 MB), wooden_bed (4 MB),
  wooden_chest (18 MB - the Chest uses treasure_chest). About 81 MB. Loot candidates?
  Keep the ones meant to become loot props, delete the rest.
- Models used only by Prefabs/Dressing (section 5): wooden_bench, wooden_broom,
  wooden_chair, wooden_chair_2, wooden_shelf (~83 MB with their unused extra maps).
